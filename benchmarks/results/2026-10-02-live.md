# Preload benchmark — 2026-10-02

Real backend at localhost:8080 and signed R2 audio, through the localhost:5173 Vite dev server. No synthetic network delay or throttling was applied. Browser HTTP cache controls were not configured; treat this as an exploratory run.

Browser: Mozilla/5.0 (Windows NT 10.0; Win64; x64) AppleWebKit/537.36 (KHTML, like Gecko) Chrome/154.0.0.0 Safari/537.36

Five tracks; 20 measured trials per scenario (60 measured selections), plus three excluded warm-ups. Scenario order rotated by round. Audio was muted. Metric: source selection to first playing event; a 1,000 ms observation window followed each start.

| Scenario | Success | Startup p50 (ms) | Startup p95 (ms) | Source p50 (ms) | Preparation p50 (ms) | Cache hits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| native | 20/20 | 1215.0 | 1427.5 | 133.2 | 0.0 | 0/20 |
| ready | 20/20 | 438.8 | 584.5 | 31.3 | 906.7 | 20/20 |
| early | 20/20 | 1228.4 | 1652.5 | 154.8 | 62.0 | 0/20 |

Ready preload reduced aggregate startup p50 by 776.2 ms (63.9%). Across paired rounds, the nearest-rank median saving was 759.9 ms.

Early selection (50 ms requested lead; observed preparation p50 62.0 ms) had zero prefix-cache hits. Its startup p50 was 13.4 ms higher than native. This run shows a ready-prefix benefit, not a speed benefit for every selection.

Ready preparation p50 was 906.7 ms, performed before selection and excluded from startup latency. Completed prefixes totalled 50.0 MB across the measured ready group (2.5 MB each). This is not total network traffic; partial preloads and native media bytes are not included.

No playback failures, duplicate signed URLs, background-start trials, or waiting events were observed in this run. A one-second window cannot establish full-song stall performance. Twenty trials per group provide only an exploratory p95.

DNS/TLS/CDN state, backend load, media cache, and scheduler variability remain uncontrolled. The harness isolates AudioPreloader and HTMLAudioElement; it excludes React rendering and physical speaker latency. Ready preload assumes a correct prediction and enough lead time.

Reproduce with the benchmark page and record environment/cache controls. Use 50–100 trials per scenario and repeat across connections before generalizing.
